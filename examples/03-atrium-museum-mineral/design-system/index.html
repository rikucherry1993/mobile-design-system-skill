Atrium Design System

Polychromie rooms — for self-guided visitors, each exhibition is painted as its own coloured room and the exhibitions are composed like the squares of a modernist wool rug (ultramarine, near-black, ecru, mustard), on ink chrome, so “which room am I in” reads at a glance and the artworks stay the richest thing. Every fill ships a contrast-checked --on-* partner; the source of truth is styles.css. Toggle Dev Mode to inspect, or ⤓ Install design system to scaffold/update it all.

Colors

One ink interaction family on a cool lavender-grey base (the rug's grey, lifted) — each fill shows its paired --on-* (top-right), WCAG-verified. The room tones are sampled from the reference rug by area (ultramarine 30% · near-black 27% · ecru 13% · mustard 11% · lavender-grey 8%): each exhibition is one square of the rug (.room-blue / .room-ink / .room-mustard / .room-ecru / .room-grey). They are content surfaces, never chrome; mustard also marks now-playing.

Typography

Inter is the reading face — body, navigation, buttons and tabular numerals (audio times, room numbers). Spectral serif is reserved for exhibition and artwork titles, like a wall label's title line.

Spacing

One 4dp-grid scale, plus fine --opt-* optical nudges. Between groups use the semantic roles: --gap-related (8) < --gap-section (24) < --gap-major (32), screen edges --layout-gutter (24), touch targets --target-min (48). Room bands are full-bleed (.bleed) with text re-inset (.inset).

Elevation

Tonal. Surfaces separate by tone (grey → surface → room); --elev-1 is none. Shadow appears only on overlays — menu, snackbar, FAB (--elev-2) and dialog (--elev-3).

Icons

Token-driven inline SVG sprite (full set in icons.html) — rounded outline, 1.75 stroke (round caps/joins to match Inter). 24px glyphs, 18px inline, 48px touch targets. Never emoji.

Imagery & mosaic

Artworks are shown whole on their room wall (.imgslot.fit, contain — never cropped; list thumbnails may crop). The images here are public-domain works from Wikimedia Commons (sources in README). A hung work gets a thin frame line — no mat texture, no shadow. The mosaic (.mosaic + .tile, cells of --mosaic-cell) is the signature layout: exhibitions placed like rug squares, filler squares in plain room tones, no gaps, no radius.
